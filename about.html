About Dry Spool Guides

Dry Spool Guides publishes buying guides for 3D-printing accessories.

How we research

  • We have not hands-on tested the products. Our guides are based on research. We say so on every page.
  • Specs come from the manufacturer's own product pages. When different official pages disagree, we tell you.
  • Performance claims, such as how accurate a dryer's temperature is, come only from independent testers we cite and link to. We don't make up measurements.
  • Prices are snapshots taken on the date shown on each guide. They change often.
  • Affiliate commissions never decide our rankings. See our disclosure.
