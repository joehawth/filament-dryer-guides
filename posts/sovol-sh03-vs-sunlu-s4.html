Sovol SH03 vs SUNLU S4

Research-based comparison: we have not tested either unit. Sources are linked below. Prices were checked on October 28, 2026.

Short answer

Both dryers hold four 1 kg spools. Choose the Sovol SH03 if you dry nylon or other engineering filaments, where its 85 °C ceiling and two separately set chambers matter [1]. Choose the SUNLU S4 if you mostly print PLA, PETG and TPU. Its temperature accuracy has independent data behind it [3], and it was often cheaper at Amazon when we checked [4].

What reviewers found

  • SH03: MyTechFun (a free unit; the channel is sponsored by Polymaker) recorded about 84–85 °C with the lid sealed, and lower readings with a gap left for the sensor wire. Criticisms: filament exits only upward, the roller spacing makes spools turn stiffly, and no desiccant is included [6]. One forum user doubted the humidity readout [7].
  • S4: measured within ±2 °C of its setpoint, with a peak draw of about 295 W. Criticisms: loud fans and poor sealing once switched off [3].

We found no independent temperature-accuracy table for the SH03 that matches the S4 test, so the two can't be compared directly on accuracy.
