Creality Space Pi vs SUNLU S4

Research-based comparison: we have not tested either unit. Sources are linked below. Prices were checked on October 28, 2026.

Short answer

If you dry more than one spool at a time, or you feed an AMS-style multi-spool system, the SUNLU S4 is the better-supported choice. It holds four spools and had much tighter temperature control in the one independent head-to-head test [3]. The Space Pi makes sense if you only need one spool, have little space, or are on a tight budget. Watch its temperature overshoot, especially with PLA [3].

Temperature accuracy

3D Print Beginner placed a probe near the top of each dryer, with filament loaded. At a 70 °C setting, the Space Pi measured 81 °C and the S4 measured 72 °C. At 65 °C, the readings were 79 °C and 65 °C [3]. All units were supplied by the manufacturers. This is a single test, and a Bambu Lab forum bake-off reported different sensor behavior on the older SUNLU S2 [5]. Treat it as one data point, not the final word.

Noise, sealing and usability

The same reviewer found both units somewhat loud and found that neither held humidity down overnight once switched off. They also reported the Space Pi's touchscreen sometimes didn't respond [3].

Verdict

SUNLU S4: check price · Creality Space Pi: check price
